## Evaluation repair

Erratum/v14a13_proposition_evaluator.py requires proposition-level reviewer annotation per family:
- status = supported, missing, contradicted or unclear;
- verbatim evidence span supporting or contradicting each factual claim;
- explicit material contradiction descriptions, supported by exact response spans.

Every required proposition must be marked supported, and no material contradiction can be present. Collision questions additionally require differentiation from the competing concept; false-premise prompts require explicit rejection and corrective causal mechanism; learning-rate prompts require distinct gradient-bound versus optimizer-step-scale mechanisms.

**This is a structured human-annotation validator, NOT an automatic truth classifier.** Reviewer labels are scientific judgments; exact evidence spans validate traceability only. A keyword match cannot establish factual correctness. The existing single assistant reviewer is exploratory and non-blind, not independently validated.

Before claiming factual progress, have at least two independent, blinded reviewers score the outputs under a locked rubric, adjudicate disagreements, and report scores per family including legacy rubric false positives. Retain repetition and EOS as separate diagnostics.

## External-reference challenge battery and rubric limits

Source documents:

- Conditional norm scaling avoids amplifying subthreshold gradients: https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/main/generated/torch.nn.utils.clip_grads_with_norm_.html
- Learning rate is an optimizer control: https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.optim.AdamW.html

The manually authored challenge set is separate from training data and contains 16 source-grounded examples: eight adequate and eight inadequate, covering all seven semantic families and a checkpoint contrast. It includes lexical bait, false equivalence, wrong clipping timing, incorrect threshold behavior, premise endorsement, and a material safety guarantee contradicting an otherwise adequate response.

Outcome on the pre-authored labels: 8 TP, 8 TN, 0 FP, 0 FN. This is a **policy-consistency test against gold annotations**. It is NOT evidence of an autonomous factual classifier with 100 percent real-world accuracy, and it is NOT independent human adjudication.

The proposition checker verifies reviewer-provided factual labels and verbatim supporting/conflicting spans; a contradiction veto applies even if all required propositions were marked supported. The two-reviewer gate requires different IDs and flags differing proposition labels or contradiction evidence for reconciliation. Reviewer independence and truthfulness cannot be established from reviewer IDs. A dishonest or mistaken reviewer can fabricate an all-supported judgment using genuine response text: that failure mode is explicitly covered by a test.

All challenge cases and training-pair corrections were authored by the assistant in this research session. Independent, blinded expert review remains outstanding; it must be conducted before claims of measured factual progress. Keep this evaluation separate from any fresh blind holdout and from training or checkpoint promotion.
