The learning-rate pair deserves priority. The explicit question asks to compare gradient clipping with a checkpoint, but the cue describes clipping versus lowering learning rate. Corrected targets answer both questions because v14a12 appends the checkpoint distinction. That is not demonstrably false supervision; it is unfocused supervision on an extremely small training panel.

Two automatic conditional-norm flags (0359 and 0366) are *false alarms* as substantive omissions. Row 0359 distinguishes reducing oversized vectors from leaving smaller ones unchanged. Row 0366 gives the mathematical conditional scaling factor min(1, threshold / norm). The current triage's lexical heuristic misses both: another reason it must never count as factual scoring.

Several original targets contain weak formulaic phrasing. Row 0223's control target includes a grammatical fragment; row 0152's control target has a malformed clause. The corrected targets are more direct in these cases. This audit does not claim that all 28 answers have independently validated factual adequacy: explicit human labels remain pending.

The four corrected false-premise *training* answers reject the incorrect premise; this must not be confused with historical *generated* output, where none of the four evaluated false-premise corrections was adequate.

## Evaluation repair

Erratum/v14a13_proposition_evaluator.py requires proposition-level reviewer annotation per family:
- status = supported, missing, contradicted or unclear;
- verbatim evidence span supporting or contradicting each factual claim;
- explicit material contradiction descriptions, supported by exact response spans.

Every required proposition must be marked supported, and no material contradiction can be present. Collision questions additionally require differentiation from the competing concept; false-premise prompts require explicit rejection and corrective causal mechanism; learning-rate prompts require distinct gradient-bound versus optimizer-step-scale mechanisms.

**This is a structured human-annotation validator, NOT an automatic truth classifier.** Reviewer labels are scientific judgments; exact evidence spans validate traceability only. A keyword match cannot establish factual correctness. The existing single assistant reviewer is exploratory and non-blind, not independently validated.

Before claiming factual progress, have at least two independent, blinded reviewers score the outputs under a locked rubric, adjudicate disagreements, and report scores per family including legacy rubric false positives. Retain repetition and EOS as separate diagnostics.
